# Blackjack 21

A classic blackjack card game for 1–6 players plus a dealer, around a 3D green-felt table with visible chip betting, a poker-style betting round (match the highest bet, raise, all in or fold), bots with personalities, a talking dealer, and 10 original music tracks synthesised in your browser.

**Status:** playable solo game against bots, with sound and music, characters, install-as-app and offline support. Online rooms with friends are next.

- Run, test and deploy: [HOW_TO_RUN.md](HOW_TO_RUN.md)
