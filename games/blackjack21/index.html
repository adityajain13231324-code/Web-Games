    How to play

    • Goal: beat the dealer's hand without going over 21.
    • Cards: 2 to 10 are face value, J/Q/K are 10, an Ace is 1 or 11.
    • Betting goes round the table. The opener bets anything up to the table maximum. Everyone after must match the highest bet, raise, or fold and sit the round out. If you have less than the bet, you must go all in with what you have, or fold. (This can be switched to free betting in the table setup.)
    • Hit takes another card. Stand keeps your hand.
    • Double down doubles your bet for exactly one more card.
    • Split turns a pair into two hands, each with its own bet.
    • Surrender gives up your first two cards for half your bet back (not available after you hit or split).
    • Insurance: when the dealer shows an Ace, you may bet half your bet that they have blackjack. It pays 2 to 1.
    • The dealer draws to 16 and stands on all 17s.
    • Pays: win 1 to 1, blackjack 3 to 2, a tie returns your bet.
    • Out of chips? Rebuy once. After the last round, the player with the most chips wins.
    • Keys: H hit, S stand, D double, P split, R surrender, Enter to confirm a bet.
