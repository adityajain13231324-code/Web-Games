      The 30-second briefing

      Trust your first guess.

      1. Create or join a room. Up to 8 players, each on their own device. Send the invite link, or try solo practice.
      2. Everyone can score. When you guess right you get points based on the timer: 100 at the start, down to 10 at the end. Other players keep guessing the same picture.
      3. Watch the reveal style. Pixels, Zoom, Tiles, Spotlight or Shuffle. Small typos and common names are accepted.

      There's no penalty for wrong guesses, so keep typing. The round ends when everyone has got it or skipped, or when time runs out. If nobody guesses your own custom pic, you get 30 points.
